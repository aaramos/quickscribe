Cleanup fallback rates, typed scripted dictations (50), both routes.

A fallback is when the model's answer fails the safety check (or errors) and
the person's tidied words are inserted instead. The service run used a local
35B instruct model, 4-bit, warmed, one request at a time.

Notes:
- The service set needs no new limits: its refusal rate sits BELOW Apple's in
  every mode. The limits tuned for Apple's model hold for a general model too.
- Every service fallback was the guard rejecting a changed number or dropping
  a required word; there were no connection or key errors in any run.
- Mode overlap: one dictation (spoken dates as words, digits in the answer)
  trips the service guard in all four modes and Apple's in none; the fallback
  inserts the person's own words either way, nothing is lost.
- Raw JSON lines: clean_apple_*.jsonl and clean_service_*.jsonl (same shape as
  the 2026-11-03 runs; score against eval/corpus.py as usual).
