Cleanup fallback rates, typed scripted dictations (50), both routes.

A fallback is when the model's answer fails the safety check (or errors) and
the person's tidied words are inserted instead. The service route ran a local
instruction model over the loopback address, warmed, one request at a time.

Notes:
- The service set needs no new limits: its refusal rate sits BELOW Apple's in
  every mode. The limits tuned for Apple's model hold for a general model too.
- Rerun after the guard learned to refuse a currency symbol the speaker never
  gave (the first service run turned "4.2 million" into "$4.2 million" and
  passed): Apple rates are unchanged to the item, and the service moved only
  by ordinary sampling variation between identical model versions. Zero
  inserted answers carry an invented currency sign.
- All failures were the guard rejecting a changed number or a dropped word;
  there were no connection or key errors in any run.
